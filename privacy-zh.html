Stairly · Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-__ (fill in on publish)

The short version

This app does not collect, upload, or share any of your personal data. All habits and check-in records are stored only on your own device.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account system, no analytics SDK, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. The app does not send your data to any server.

Where your data lives

Your habits, check-in records, notes, and settings are kept in on-device local storage, accessible only to you. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes them.

Permissions

  • Notifications: used only to deliver local check-in reminders that you explicitly set for a habit. Reminders are generated on-device and never travel over the network.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no age information.
