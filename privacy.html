Stairly — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2026 · Applies to the Stairly app on all platforms (iOS, HarmonyOS)

1. The short version

Stairly stores everything on your device. We do not collect, upload, or share any personal data. There is no server, no account system, and no analytics SDK. Your habits are yours.

2. What data exists, and where

All data you create in Stairly — habits, check-in records, fail logs, notes, and settings — is saved only in your device's local storage (iOS local storage / HarmonyOS Preferences). It never leaves your device.

  • We do not have a backend server. There is nothing to send data to.
  • We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not collect device identifiers, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.

3. Permissions

Stairly may request the following permission, only if you use the related feature:

  • Notifications — used solely for habit reminders you configure. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device; no push service is involved.

You may decline or revoke these permissions at any time in system Settings; the rest of the app will continue to work.

4. Data export & deletion

You can export a full backup of your data as a JSON file at any time (Me → Backup & Restore), and you can erase all data in-app (Me → Backup & Restore → Erase all data). Uninstalling the app removes all local data permanently.

5. Children

Stairly is a general-purpose habit tool and does not direct itself to children under 13. Since we collect no data at all, no data of children is processed.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds optional cloud sync), we will update this page and the in-app notice before the change takes effect.
